## Principles for v4

1. **Photography of people carries every chapter.** Real FASTR moments from Instagram
   first, editorial licensed stock second (quality of the first four images, no brand
   logos), Fabi's solo footage only as a small detail if at all. No chapter is carried by
   a type plate.
2. **Fewer, bigger moments.** Fewer sections, more air. Some chapters are just an image
   and one line, like SOM. Cut copy by about 40 %; no paragraph longer than two lines on
   desktop.
3. **The temple as an editorial mosaic, not strips.** Keep the inscriptions OWN GREATNESS
   above and ORIGINAL GRIND below, but the five pillars become large photographs: row one
   two big tiles (Run, Practice), row two three tiles (Wear, Meet, Make), each with the
   pillar name small in a corner (SOM's ARCHIVE / WORKWEAR / FEATURED grammar), slow zoom
   on hover. Phone: one tile per row, full width, 4:5.
4. **Colour as accent, not surface.** Lime and lavender as thin rules, numerals, labels,
   the FASTR30 band. No full lime or lavender plates except the band.
5. **One loud headline per screen, not per section.** Huge type only where it earns it
   (opening, manifest, one per pillar at most).
