# OG SPORTS — benchmark-led art direction / Review 3

## Purpose and method

The request is to raise OG SPORTS to the standing of a distinctive international brand, not to imitate a motorsport brand or copy a fashionable agency template. The working principle remains: **außergewöhnlich, aber clean**.

This is a qualitative design review, not an objective ranking of the world's best websites. Official pages were read and browser captures were inspected during this iteration. Observations distinguish visible design choices from our judgement about their relevance to OG SPORTS. No conversion, loading-speed, accessibility or business-performance claim is inferred from visual inspection.

The main reference set spans fashion/community, musical hardware, consumer technology, luxury hospitality, photography and financial software. SOM means **SOM Studios / PROJECT50**, not the unrelated creative studio at som.studio. Both names appeared during discovery; the PROJECT50 connection establishes the relevant reference.

## 1. SOM Studios / PROJECT50 — fashion and community

Sources: https://somstudios.com/ and https://somstudios.com/collections/project-50

**What is distinctive:** Clothing, PROJECT50, the Creators Club and events belong to one visible cultural world. The homepage is led by atmospheric human imagery, with a relatively restrained brand header. The culture makes the product feel less isolated.

**Useful to OG:** OG SPORTS needs to be understandable as a parent brand, with FASTR and the initiatives expressing the same culture. Show the relationship between movement, people and making instead of presenting four unrelated mock campaigns.

**Friction / not copied:** The visible consent layer interrupts the first impression in the inspected session. Dense nested shop/navigation choices and a commerce-first product listing would overstate our current offering. We do not have a released catalogue to present as a functioning shop.

**Applied:** A new open brand chapter links Move, Meet and Make to actual sections or project views. These ideas are no longer hidden in a second set of tabs. Development labels remain truthful.

## 2. Teenage Engineering / OP–1 field — musical hardware

Source: https://teenage.engineering/products/op-1

**What is distinctive:** A product-led visual identity: restrained composition, unusual scale, close product inspection and precise technical descriptions. The interface looks related to the objects rather than to a generic ecommerce theme.

**Useful to OG:** Give each initiative an appropriate presentation. The daily-practice format, clothing reference, live-culture concept and prototype should not all use the same poster template.

**Friction / not copied:** The page contains a very extensive feature inventory and compact navigation/details. That specificity can serve expert buyers but would be excessive on our parent-brand homepage. We also cannot substitute polished fake hardware for a real prototype.

**Applied:** FASTR30 stays a graphic practice poster; Apparel is the broad photographic story; Live becomes an immersive image; STICK remains an explicitly unfinished, restrained typographic project. No invented engineering performance or finished product render.

## 3. Apple / AirPods Pro — consumer technology

Source: https://www.apple.com/airpods-pro/

**What is distinctive:** The page moves from a clear promise and large product view to highlights and closer inspection. Scale and sequencing establish hierarchy before the visitor encounters all the technical depth.

**Useful to OG:** A homepage should be a deliberately ordered story, not a sequence of equally emphatic modules. Let a visitor feel the brand, understand it, discover the work and then act.

**Friction / not copied:** Large media, long feature journeys and many card-based highlights are costly to imitate poorly. A rounded-card product page or elaborate scroll choreography would not by itself give OG a unique identity.

**Applied:** Clear chapter progression: the existing emotional opening; a concise parent-brand statement; the FASTR movement; four differently weighted initiatives; one decisive closing invitation. Native scrolling is retained; no scroll lock, new loading gate or mandatory animation.

## 4. Aman / Amangiri — luxury hospitality

Sources: https://www.aman.com/ and https://www.aman.com/resorts/amangiri

**What is distinctive:** Place-led photography, restrained chrome, measured spacing and a deliberate pace. Image and atmosphere do much of the work rather than constant interface decoration.

**Useful to OG:** Empty space must frame something worth looking at. Preserve meaningful image proportions and separate explanatory information from the image itself.

**Friction / not copied:** The inspected pages use prominent consent UI and video-led presentation; the resort page also has many navigation categories. The refined serif/resort aesthetic and passive mood are not our sport-culture identity. No conclusion about general video performance is drawn from the inspected session.

**Applied:** The cycling photo remains at its full aspect ratio; its reference/non-OG-product notice moves outside the image. Repeated decorative corners are removed from gallery artwork. The running chapter pairs a large photograph with a shorter, lower-positioned text group.

## 5. Leica — photography and a wider brand ecosystem

Source: https://leica-camera.com/en-int

**What is distinctive:** Cameras sit beside photography, galleries, education, stories and events. The brand communicates a culture and an activity, not only a set of products.

**Useful to OG:** Connect product, experience and community through a shared point of view. Large imagery should carry a chapter's meaning; text should identify what is actually offered.

**Friction / not copied:** The breadth of an established ecosystem produces many competing paths. OG should not imitate that volume or invent evidence of scale, partnerships or heritage.

**Applied:** The manifesto says what we are building. Live gets a clear photographic moment, while its development and AI-study status remain outside the image. OG SPORTS signs the whole journey with its existing original mark, rather than another generic footer block.

## 6. Stripe — financial software

Source: https://stripe.com/

**What is distinctive:** The page pairs a direct explanation of the offering with concrete product surfaces, customer examples and several defined entry paths. A complex platform is made more inspectable through specifics.

**Useful to OG:** Visitors should understand the next action. Join, create and partner are different intentions and should remain distinct.

**Friction / not copied:** The density of product categories, technical detail, numerical proof and animated graphics serves a different buying context. Borrowing the gradients, dashboards or business claims would make our site generic and misleading.

**Applied:** The closing chapter uses three clear existing paths, with a shorter introduction and no additional form features. Contact still creates a local text draft, not a sent enquiry. No fabricated statistics or partner logos.

## References excluded from visual conclusions

Aesop and Gentle Monster were also opened. Their content could be retrieved in text, but the browser captures reached verification/403 pages. They were not treated as visually audited references and no attempt was made to bypass those restrictions.

## Diagnosis of our previous version

- The opening had a recognizable image/type relationship, but the rest repeated similar two-column statements.
- The second Move/Meet/Make tab group hid basic brand information behind unnecessary interaction.
- Open corners had become a repeated decoration instead of a selective signature.
- Live looked like a picture nested inside another mock poster, not a distinct cultural moment.
- The closing text box did not give the original identity enough presence.

## Implemented design direction

**Common ground, independent expression.** One recognizable visual system, several appropriately different compositions.

- Preserve the chosen first Meet image and every original logo asset byte for byte.
- Replace the secondary brand tabs with a large bilingual statement and three visible principles.
- Clarify copy without asserting a market position, successful event or released product.
- Preserve the established asymmetric project grid and full cycling-photo ratio.
- Use one large immersive Live image, with a clear white title and visible AI-study/development notice.
- Remove repeated corners from gallery covers; retain the original opening and running-image frame language.
- Form one continuous lilac closing plane, with three actions and the original OG SPORTS wordmark at architectural scale.
- Keep DE/EN, visible focus, reduced-motion behaviour, local photographs and truthful contact handling.

## Limits and next design evidence

The comparison does not prove that the result matches the reference brands' quality or achieves better conversion. Authentic OG campaign photography, actual material/product studies and real community stories remain the most valuable missing inputs. Stock reference photos and identified AI studies cannot substitute for a documented culture indefinitely.

Review screenshots come from the actual implementation, not a generated website mockup. Local inline renders are labelled as such. The GitHub review workflow tests the exact HTTP-served build before committing the applied changes; its result must be checked rather than assumed. Chromium tests do not establish Safari/Firefox/real-device or complete accessibility coverage.
