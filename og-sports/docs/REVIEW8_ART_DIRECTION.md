# OG SPORTS / Review 8 — Field Atlas

**Working creative direction:** extraordinary, but clean. A coherent sport-and-culture world rather than a sequence of generic website blocks.

## Decisions inherited from user feedback
- Retain the selected first Meet image as our lead visual. The larger interpolated file remains a derivative, not verified AI super-resolution.
- Retain exactly the next three approved editorial photographs and their Unsplash credit links.
- Do not reintroduce the previously rejected runner, cycling, rooftop or community photos in lower homepage sections.
- Do not imitate F1's sport or appearance: the reference is its standing and brand confidence.
- Stay close to the cultural/editorial staging the user admires at SOM Studios / PROJECT50, but make a distinct OG visual grammar.

## R8 art direction
### Motion Atlas / OG FASTR
A bespoke SVG field illustration made of twelve fine trajectories plus one acid-chartreuse connection line. It provides a visual metaphor for independent paths meeting through movement. The curve is not a racing circuit. It sits behind the movement campaign typography; reduced-motion settings stop its almost imperceptible drift. Existing OG FASTR master SVG remains unchanged.

### Wienerwald colour research / Apparel
Four original, source-editable geometric colour studies (Apple Sky, Pear Moss, Rosehip Stone and Black Form), presented as a research wall. These are colour-direction illustrations, not photographs of released jerseys. They respect the user's preference for geometry over decorative fruit motifs. Their colours are provisional explorations, not manufacturer-approved Pantone/production specifications.

### Collective signal / Live Experiences
A static geometric pulse field with concentric arcs, nodes and a single connecting curve. Visual research only: it is not documentation of an OG event or proof that tickets, performers or partners have been secured.

### Object schematic / FASTR STICK
A standalone concept illustration of a slim cylindrical 30 ml stick and a generic internal feed path. It is **not** the validated V0.18.5 CAD model, an actual engineering section, or a manufactured product. Technical claims must derive from the real CAD programme, not this art-direction sketch.

### Contact / Identity
One large lightly embossed OG signet treatment in the invitation closure. The original signet SVG is reused without changing its paths; it is purely decorative.

## What we removed from the R7 look
- The flat 3-bar apparel accent without research labels.
- An empty dark Live block with only a headline.
- The schematic-free 30 ml number beside FASTR STICK.
- Redundant large homogeneous headlines where an original image field can communicate the idea.

## Source and rights
The new SVGs are authored for this website and contain no third-party photographs, brand logos or external font files. Original OG/FASTR assets have not been edited. Existing real photos are references only, not OG personnel or campaigns; commercial clearances remain outstanding.

## Review boundary
This is a **design review on a draft GitHub branch**, not a public brand launch. Any apparel shades, event visuals and product dimensions beyond the confirmed 30 ml capacity remain conceptual. The on-page contact flow generates a local text draft only. No analytics, payment, emailing or booking backend was added.

**Test scope:** Node build/source checks; Chromium screenshot and interaction checks over seven widths, with external requests blocked. Safari, Firefox, real device and full accessibility audits remain separate launch work.
